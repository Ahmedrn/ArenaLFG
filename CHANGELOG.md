# Changelog

## 2.4.0 (2026-10-30)

- Players on an older version now get their race and faction icon from a quiet /who (own faction only; can be switched off in the options).
- New option, off by default: advertise ArenaLFG in Global chat every 5 minutes.
- Listings from players on an older version now show their spec in your language instead of "None" or a foreign name.
- Signing up to a listing taken from Global chat opens a whisper already written with your spec, gear score and rating, ending with "(ArenaLFG)".
- One Skom's minimap button opens all your Skom's addons when two or more are installed (/skoms).
- New **Arena Stats** button when Skom's Arena Stats is installed.

## 2.3.1

- The options window now opens in front of ArenaLFG.
- Updated About page.
- Global ads now show when the player is a seasonal character, and recognize "ench" for Enhancement Shaman.

## 2.3.0

- Your listing follows your team and rating automatically, no more relisting.
- Better recognition of paladin ads in Global chat.

## 2.2.0

- Group title filled in from your choices, with an optional separate message for Global chat.
- About page and *Copy armory link*.

## 2.1.0

- The Global announcement shows your group title.

## 2.0.0

- **3v3 and DuoQ brackets**, with comp picking in 3v3.
- Applicants with rating and gearscore, sortable, and *Send application*.
- Friends, guildmates and players who declined you are highlighted; *Looking for my spec* filter; hide players.
- Auto-reply while you are in an arena, and listings stay up in queue.
- Full options page with profiles and alert sounds.

## 1.4.0

## 1.2.0
